## 🌟 Key Benefits

- **Instant Communication**: Receive real-time notifications about maintenance, water supply, and society announcements.
- **Efficient Complaint Management**: Lodge complaints in seconds and track their status from "Pending" to "Resolved" without visiting the office.
- **Transparency**: Clear visibility of water supply schedules and official society notices.
- **Privacy & Security**: Secure login system with dedicated roles for Residents, Admins, and Super Admins.
- **Feedback Loop**: Rate the resolution of your complaints to help management improve services.

## 🚀 Main Features

### 👤 User Roles
- **Resident**: Can lodge complaints, track history, view water schedules, and react to announcements.
- **Admin**: Manages complaints, schedules water supply, posts announcements, and approves new users.
- **Super Admin**: Full control over the system, including managing other admins and high-level settings.

### 📋 Feature Modules
1. **Notification Center**: A dedicated hub for personal and society-wide alerts. Includes high-priority "Maintenance Due" alerts that stay visible until acknowledged.
2. **Complaint Portal**:
   - Categorized complaint lodging (Maintenance, Water, Security, etc.).
   - Unique reference numbers for tracking.
   - Interactive rating system for resolved complaints.
3. **Water Supply Schedule**:
   - List view of upcoming supply timings.
   - Integrated **Calendar View** to check schedules for the entire month.
4. **Society Announcements**:
   - Official updates with "Reaction" support (👍, ❤️, 😂).
   - Easy sharing of announcements to other platforms (WhatsApp, Email, etc.).
5. **Admin Dashboard**:
   - User approval system to prevent unauthorized access.
   - Database export (CSV) for record-keeping.
   - Admin management for the Super Admin.

## 📖 User Guide

### 1. Getting Started
- **Welcome Screen**: Tap "Enter Portal" to begin.
- **Registration**: New residents must register with their Name, Phone Number, CNIC, and House Number.
- **Approval**: After registration, your account will be "Pending Approval." An admin will verify your details to grant access.

### 2. Logging In
- **Resident**: Use your House Number and Phone Number.
- **Options**:
  - **Remember House Number**: Saves your ID for the next time.
  - **Keep me logged in**: Skips the login screen on future app opens.
  - **Password Toggle**: Tap the "eye" icon to show/hide your password.

### 3. Using the App
- **Bottom Navigation**: Easily switch between **Notifications**, **Complaints**, **Water**, and **Announcements**.
- **Theming**: Tap the palette icon in the top bar to switch between Light, Dark, and various color themes.
- **Logout**: Use the red Logout button in the top bar to securely exit your session.
